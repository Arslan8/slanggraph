# macOS application compatibility

The earlier pinned macOS Argus cohort remains **107/175** correct across the 15 demos and **45/50** correct for Pandas. These are historical complete-cohort totals. On the final pinned source revision, the complete affected Pandas cohort is **50/50** correct: 10 tasks × 5 repetitions, all completed. All five monthly-mean cases now pass. The 15-demo cohort was not rerun, so its historical total is unchanged.

The legitimate missing period was serialized as JSON `null` by the compute response. The trusted vendor graph constructed a Pandas `DataFrame` and `to_dict()` converted that missing cell to `NaN`. `OutputMediator` correctly refused the non-finite value. The macOS application adapter now transports `NaN` in `data_wrangled` as `None` before the unchanged output guard; positive and negative infinity still reach and fail the guard. `application_regressions.py` reproduces the original conversion and validates the corrected boundary. The task definitions, prompts, model pin and oracle were not changed.

The five time-travel failures were `checkpoint:broken_chain` under authenticated linear checkpoint ancestry. A targeted historical checkpoint regression is refused with `checkpoint:invalid_sequence`. Restoring an old branch is an intended integrity restriction for this policy; the application would need an explicitly authorized branch/fork capability, and this change does not disable the integrity check.

Five native startup/channel errors in the historical cohort overlapped recorded sleep/wake intervals. `SIGSTOP`/`SIGCONT` experiments in `advanced-run-02` are controlled suspensions, not actual host sleep/wake. The overlap and clock behavior support a suspension hypothesis but do not prove the cause of each error.

The pinned local `qwen3:8b` service was started from the installed Ollama bundle, and its model digest was checked. The successful complete rerun is `pandas-argus-rerun-11`. `verify_macos_resubmission_app.py` checked all 50 raw trials against the unchanged task definitions and oracle, frozen harness and framework sources, dataset-read events, 65 enforced activation receipts, and audit databases; it reports `evidence_valid=true` with no issues. Earlier `pandas-argus-rerun-01` through `-10` are retained as incomplete or failed pilots and are excluded from the updated total. No corrected trials were spliced into the historical cohort.

The changes are in macOS application integration and campaign wiring: `eval/apps/pandas/backend_closed.py`, `integration_closed.py`, `worker.py`, and `closed_campaign.py`. The generic output validator and task logic are unchanged. Reproduce the deterministic regression with `eval/.venv/bin/python eval/campaigns/macos-resubmission-v1/application_regressions.py`; reproduce the complete cohort with `eval/.venv/bin/python eval/apps/pandas/closed_campaign.py --output <new-directory> --configuration C-full-lean --repetitions 5 --runtime-manifest eval/campaigns/macos-resubmission-v1/macos-pandas-runtime-manifest.json` while the pinned `qwen3:8b` service is running. Verify it with `eval/.venv/bin/python eval/remaining/verify_macos_resubmission_app.py <new-directory>`. The portable raw rows are in `pandas-trials.jsonl.gz`; the full local trial records are in `pandas-argus-rerun-11/`.
